The UK Government AI Strategy outlines a comprehensive approach to artificial 
intelligence adoption across public services. The strategy identifies three 
core pillars: safety, innovation, and inclusion.

Safety requirements mandate that all government AI systems undergo independent 
audit before deployment. Systems that make decisions affecting individuals must 
maintain human oversight and provide explainable outputs.

The Home Office AI programme has delivered significant improvements in visa 
processing times, reducing average decisions from 30 days to 8 days through 
intelligent document verification and risk assessment models.

Budget allocation for AI across government stands at £2.5 billion over the 
next three years, with 40% dedicated to NHS applications, 30% to border 
security, and 30% to administrative efficiency programmes.
